## 📝 About the Project

Indonesian cuisine is rich, diverse, and full of unique cultural flavors — yet, many local dishes remain undiscovered by the younger generation. **Dishcovery App** is built to bridge this gap by providing an engaging way for users to **explore, learn, and rediscover traditional Indonesian dishes** through a modern mobile experience powered by **Flutter** and **Firebase**.

We aim to preserve Indonesia’s culinary heritage by bringing technology closer to local food discovery.

## 🧩 **Project Highlights**

### 🚀 How We Got Here
Our team started with a shared passion for Indonesian cuisine and technology. We noticed that many people struggle to find traditional dishes or understand their origins. That insight led to **Dishcovery** — an app that uses data, AI, and community interaction to help users explore culinary heritage in a fun, personalized way.

### 🛠️ Scope
- **Dish Recommendation System**  
  - Machine learning–based recommendation using ingredient and preference matching.
  - Suggests related dishes based on user interests and search history.

- **Features for Users**  
  - **Explore Dishes**: Browse Indonesian cuisines by region, taste, or category.  
  - **Favorites**: Save your favorite dishes for quick access.  
  - **Search & Filter**: Quickly find specific dishes or ingredients.  
  - **Detail Page**: View recipe, ingredients, history, and cultural background.  
  - **Recommendation Engine**: Personalized dish suggestions based on your activity.  

- **Secure Cloud Integration**  
  - User preferences, favorites, and data stored securely via Firebase and Supabase.

### 📦 Deliverables
- A mobile application that allows users to:  
  - Explore Indonesian culinary heritage interactively.  
  - Discover traditional dishes and their historical background.  
  - Save and share favorite dishes.  
  - Get AI-based recommendations.  
  - Experience seamless integration with cloud and machine learning.  

---

## 🛠️ **Technology Stack**
- **Machine Learning**: Dish recommendation model using content-based filtering.  
- **Mobile Development**: Flutter application for Android and iOS.  
- **Cloud Computing**: Firebase & Supabase for authentication and data management.
